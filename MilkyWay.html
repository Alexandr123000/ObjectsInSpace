Milky Way

The Milky Way is a spiral galaxy and contains our Solar System. The structure of this galaxy is as follows:

  1. Core
    2. The Milky Way contains a supermassive black hole at its center.
  2. Disk
    3. The disk is a flat structure that contains most of the stars.
  3. Bulge
    4. The core surrounds a spheroidal cluster of stars. The bulge consists mostly of old stars and has a high density.
  4. Bar
    5. The bar crosses the center of the Milky Way and connects the pirral arms.
  5. Spiral Arms
    6. The galaxy has several spiral arms, including: Orion Arm, Cygnus Arm, Perseus Arm.
  6. Halo
    7. The halo is a spheroidal region surrounding the galactic disk. This region contains globular clusters, dark matter, and old stars.
  7. Dark matter
    8. Dark matter is an invisible substance that makes up a significant postion of the galaxy's mass. It exerts a gravitational pull on visible matter and emits no light.
