Sun

It's worth nothing that its diameter is 109 times greater than Earth's, at 1,392,020 km. The star's surface is extremely hot, but its core, specifically, has a temperture of 15m. Kelvin. Ligth takes 8 minutes to reach Earth's surface, which is quite slow, but considering the distance and speed of light, this becomes obvious. It's also worth nothing that the Sun is many times larger than the largest planet in the Solar System, Jupiter.
