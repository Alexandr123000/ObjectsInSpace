Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun. It is a realtively harsh and cold planet. Mars has a high density and is also characterized by a low mass. Its surface is composed of rock, while its core is composed of iron. The average distance from Mars to the Sun is approximately 228 m. km.. Surface temperatures on Mars range from 20°C to 153°C. It is worth noting that a day on Mars lasts 24 hours, 39 minutes, and 35 seconds.
